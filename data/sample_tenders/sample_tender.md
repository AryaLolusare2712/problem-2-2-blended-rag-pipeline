# Sample Tender: Municipal Fleet Maintenance Services
Tender ID: MFS/PROC/2026/017
Issuing authority: Example Municipal Services Unit
Issue date: 14 October 2026
Submission deadline: 30 November 2026, 17:00 IST

## Eligibility and financial capacity
The bidder must have completed at least three comparable fleet-maintenance contracts during the previous five financial years. The aggregate value of those contracts must be at least INR 1.5 crore. The bidder must demonstrate average annual turnover of at least INR 2 crore in the last three audited financial years.

## Bid security
Bid security is INR 5,00,000. The security instrument must remain valid for 45 days beyond the bid validity period. Exemptions, if any, must be supported by a valid certificate and reviewed by the procurement officer.

## Technical requirements
The service provider must provide preventive maintenance, emergency roadside support, and monthly vehicle condition reports. Critical breakdowns must receive an initial response within 90 minutes in the service area.

## Evaluation
Technical qualification is assessed before financial bids are opened. The financial proposal is evaluated only for bidders who meet the mandatory eligibility criteria. The authority may seek clarification but will not permit a material change to the submitted bid.
